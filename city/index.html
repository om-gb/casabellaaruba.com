Introduction

City living, turned inward.

Casabella City gathers its apartments around one long, landscaped courtyard. From the street it is quiet stone and white render. Inside the walls you find palms, a lit pool and covered dining terraces.

Every apartment opens onto a shaded gallery that runs the length of each wing. That keeps the bedrooms on the outer side and the living areas toward the courtyard. It suits a first home in town, a second home on the island, or a long-term rental.

Location · San Nicolas

Aruba’s second city, close to its best beaches.

Casabella City is in San Nicolas, on the quiet south-east side of the island. The murals of the downtown street-art district and Charlie’s Bar are a few minutes away.

Baby Beach and Rodgers Beach on the south-east tip, and the fresh catch at Zeerovers in Savaneta, are all within a quarter of an hour, and the airport is about 20 minutes up the coast road.
