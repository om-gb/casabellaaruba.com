The rooms

Calm, warm, simple.

Two floors of suites over covered parking, a lift and stairs to every level, and a planted roof terrace on top. Each suite has a full bathroom, built-in closets and a balcony; the two-bedroom suites add a second bedroom and bathroom. Finishes are still being chosen — the pictures show the direction.
