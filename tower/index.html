The Tower

A small building on the best-walked street in Aruba

Casabella Tower is a low-rise in Palm Beach with four residences to a floor, topped by three-level loft homes with private roof terraces.

The architecture is deliberately quiet: white volumes stepped back as the building rises, deep planted balconies that shade the glass through the afternoon, and a timber-screened stair core that carries light down the west façade after dark. Floor-to-ceiling sliding glass opens every living room onto its terrace, so the boundary between inside and outside is a choice rather than a wall.

Residences

Four residences to a floor

Twelve two-bedroom residences in four layouts, each with two bathrooms and private balconies — rising to three-level loft residences with internal stairs and private roof terraces. Four homes per floor means no long corridors and no residence that faces only its neighbours. Seventeen residences in total, across five levels.

Amenities

Kept for residents

A private pool, a rooftop gym, cabanas and a west-facing terrace — all of it kept for the building. The beach, the restaurants and the crowds stay five minutes away, for when you want them.

  • 01

    Private pool

    A pool reserved for residents and their guests, away from the crowds on the beach five minutes away.

  • 02

    Rooftop gym

    A residents' gym on the roof, so the view does the work of a motivational poster.

  • 03

    Rooftop cabanas

    Shaded cabanas on the roof terrace — the good spot to watch the sun go down, kept for the building.

  • 04

    Roof terrace

    More than 145 m² of shared terrace crowns the building, facing west into the sunset.

  • 05

    Gazebo & BBQ areas

    A shaded gazebo and barbecue areas at ground level — for long lunches and evenings that run late.

  • 06

    Secured basement parking

    Fourteen secured parking spaces in the basement — rare on this stretch of Palm Beach, and worth more every year.

  • 07

    Deep planted balconies

    Every residence has its own balcony, planted and set deep enough to shade the glass through the afternoon.

  • 08

    Rooftop solar array

    Photovoltaic panels on the roof offset the building's common-area load — lower running costs, quietly.
